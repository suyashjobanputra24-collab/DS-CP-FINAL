💡 The 28-Second Secret: Why Keeping Promises Beats Rushing

Many food delivery managers wrongly believe that shaving 2 minutes off driving time automatically makes customers delighted. Our data proves that 5-star orders took 24.16 minutes, while 3-star orders took 24.64 minutes — a difference of just 28 seconds!

Average Transit Time by Customer Star Rating:

  • ★ 5 Stars: 24.16 mins
  • ★ 4 Stars: 24.32 mins
  • ★ 3 Stars: 24.64 mins

Notice: Transit time is virtually identical across rating tiers ($p > 0.65$).

What actually makes customers angry? Unannounced delays! A customer is perfectly happy waiting 38 minutes when told 35–42 minutes honestly. But if told 30 minutes and forced to wait 45 minutes with zero communication, they give 1 star and demand a refund. Managing expectations is 10x more important than raw speed.
