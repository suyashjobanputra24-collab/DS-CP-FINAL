⚠️ Academic Reality Check: Can Delivery Transit Time Alone Predict Satisfaction?

A common misconception in student data science projects is assuming that faster transit time automatically guarantees high customer satisfaction ratings. Our rigorous evaluation on genuine post-delivery ratings in the FoodHub dataset revealed that transit duration has almost zero correlation ($\rho = -0.01$) with customer ratings.

Empirical Mean Transit Time by Rating:

  • ★ 5 Stars: 24.16 min
  • ★ 4 Stars: 24.32 min
  • ★ 3 Stars: 24.64 min

The 28-second difference between 5-star and 3-star ratings is statistically insignificant ($p > 0.65$).

Why? Customer satisfaction is multi-factorial: food temperature upon arrival, correct items, kitchen taste, packaging integrity, and order presentation dominate customer sentiment. An order delivered in 15 minutes is still rated 1-star if the curry spilled or items were missing.
