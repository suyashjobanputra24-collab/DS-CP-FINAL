💡 Epistemic Safeguard: Mathematical Attribution vs Real-World Causality

In academic Data Science, we must strictly distinguish between predictive attribution and physical cause:

  • What SHAP tells us: SHAP values explain *how the ensemble combines feature inputs to alter its output relative to the expected base value*. A high positive SHAP value for Jam traffic indicates that the model shifts predicted delivery outward by +6.2 minutes.
  • What SHAP does NOT prove: It does not prove that road congestion caused the entire delay. In real food delivery, traffic heavily correlates with kitchen dinner surges and longer courier assignment delays. Attributing the entire delay to traffic would ignore systemic platform queuing.
  • Operational Fairness: Never use SHAP or feature importance to penalize individual couriers or restaurants; errors often stem from unmeasured environmental bottlenecks.
