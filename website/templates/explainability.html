🛡️ Fairness Guarantee: Stop Unfairly Blaming Delivery Riders

In food delivery operations, aggregators often mistakenly penalize delivery partners when orders are late. PromiseGuard's AI transparently isolates the true causes:

  • Separating Kitchen Wait from Transit: If a restaurant takes 18 minutes to cook biryani, the courier shouldn't be penalized for a 5-minute road transit.
  • Weather & Traffic Transparency: When monsoon rains gridlock arterial corridors, PromiseGuard automatically expands customer delivery windows and protects driver ratings.
  • Customer Honesty: Customers appreciate knowing why an order is delayed (e.g., "Kitchen is plating your meal with care") rather than staring at a frozen GPS map.
