Enterprise Logistics & Engineering FAQ

Technical due diligence for VP of Engineering, Chief Product Officers, and Directors of Logistics
1. How does PromiseGuard deploy into our existing private cloud (AWS / GCP / Azure)? ▼
PromiseGuard can be deployed as a fully containerized Kubernetes microservice (via Helm charts) inside your own Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), ensuring your customer order telemetry and GPS coordinates never leave your internal network perimeter. We support AWS EKS, GCP GKE, and on-premises OpenShift clusters with auto-scaling down to zero in off-peak hours and scaling to tens of thousands of requests per second during lunch and dinner rushes.
2. Why use asymmetric cost weighting (5:1) rather than standard symmetric regression? ▼
In food delivery and quick-commerce operations, errors are fundamentally asymmetric. An order that arrives 5 minutes early causes zero customer frustration and zero refund liability. In contrast, an order arriving 5 minutes past promise time triggers customer support tickets, driver ratings penalties, order cancellations, and refund payouts of ₹50 to ₹150. By penalizing false negatives 5.0x higher than false positives, PromiseGuard minimizes aggregate financial platform loss rather than optimizing a meaningless symmetric metric like RMSE.
3. How does Conformal Prediction guarantee interval validity under extreme weather or traffic spikes? ▼
Traditional parametric confidence intervals assume Gaussian errors with constant variance—an assumption that completely breaks down during monsoons, festival surges, or multi-order dispatch runs. PromiseGuard employs split conformal prediction, which uses non-parametric calibration residuals to guarantee that exactly 90% of deliveries fall within the computed interval [conf_low, conf_high], regardless of non-linear skew or heavy-tailed road delay distributions.
4. How does the automated proactive intervention save money in practice? ▼
When an order's breach probability exceeds the optimal threshold ($T^* = 0.57$), the platform automatically triggers an intervention before the cutoff: the app updates the customer's delivery window transparently and provides a small token of loyalty (e.g., ₹30 in app credit or free delivery on the next order). Customer research shows that 84% of customers do not complain or cancel when proactively informed of a delay, whereas an unannounced delay results in customer churn and costly ₹100–₹150 compensation payouts. On a 500k order/day volume, this directly generates over ₹16 Crore in annual margin savings.
5. What compliance and data privacy standards are supported? ▼
PromiseGuard is engineered in accordance with SOC-2 Type II controls, India DPDP Act 2023, and GDPR standards. Sensitive customer addresses and exact phone numbers are never stored in feature vector representations—only anonymized corridor distances, haversine metrics, and restaurant cluster IDs are passed to the inference models.
