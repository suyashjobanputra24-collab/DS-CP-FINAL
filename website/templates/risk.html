🎯 Why a 0.50 Threshold is Not Automatically Optimal

In food delivery operations, missing a severe delay is far more costly than sending an unnecessary delay notification. A customer whose meal arrives 20 minutes late without warning is likely to open a dispute, request refunds, or churn. By conducting validation-set cost curves, PromiseGuard selected $T^* = 0.57$, which achieves 90.7% recall (catching 399 out of 440 delayed orders) while maintaining a high precision of 54.1% to avoid customer alert fatigue.
