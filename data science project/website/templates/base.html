🛡️ PromiseGuard Framework

An academic research framework investigating food delivery ETA prediction reliability, multi-stage uncertainty quantification, delivery SLA breach classification, and offline proactive intervention policies.

Built with Python, Scikit-Learn, HistGradientBoosting, Split-Conformal Prediction, SHAP, and FastAPI.
